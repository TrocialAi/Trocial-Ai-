# Trocial — AI Recommendation Intelligence

**Understand how AI discovers, represents, cites, and recommends your brand.**

[Trocial](https://www.trocial.com) is an AI Recommendation Intelligence platform that helps businesses measure and improve their visibility across AI-powered search and recommendation experiences.

## What Trocial Helps You Understand

- **AI Visibility:** Understand how visible your brand is across supported AI systems.
- **Recommendation Share:** Assess how frequently your brand is recommended relative to competitors.
- **Competitive Position:** Identify where your brand stands against competing businesses.
- **Citation Intelligence:** Understand which sources influence how AI systems represent your brand.
- **Brand Accuracy:** Identify potential inaccuracies in AI-generated descriptions of your business.
- **Priority Actions:** Find opportunities to improve brand discoverability and representation.

## Who Trocial Serves

Trocial supports startups, SaaS companies, FinTech businesses, marketing teams, agencies, and enterprises globally, with a particular focus on Southeast Asia.
